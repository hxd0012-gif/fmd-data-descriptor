# Measurement and interpretation

The source is outpatient and inpatient data from The First Affiliated Hospital of Fujian Medical University, including healthy controls, prehypertension and hypertension groups. The author confirms standardized measurements by fixed trained personnel and measurement quality control. D0 is the baseline diameter at the current examination. FMD is `100*(D1-D0)/D0` and NID is `100*(D2-D0)/D0`; the author-confirmed nitroglycerin dose is 0.5 mg. Unavailable historical procedural details must be obtained from the data custodian; they are not inferred by this code.

The index record follows the existing curated selection: earliest reliably dated examination, or a more complete record where dates cannot be determined. Retain the original `visit_timing` categories. Same-day additional measurements are not different-date follow-up. Records with unknown dates may contribute measurements but cannot define elapsed time or trajectories. Do not infer chronological order from an undated sequence value.

BMI uses kg/(m squared). BSA follows the historical database formula `0.0061*height_cm+0.0128*weight_kg-0.1529`; this is a documented source convention rather than a recommendation of a preferred BSA equation. LVM uses `0.8*1.04*((LVDD+IVST+LVPWT)^3-LVDD^3)+0.6` with dimensions in cm; LVMI and cardiac index divide by that BSA. Formula inputs are examination-specific, must be finite and must have no detection qualifier. Missing inputs or invalid required denominators produce missing outputs. No patient is excluded for a particular model's complete-case requirements.

Medication names represent closed-list extraction of literal names, without validated dose, timing, duration, indication or use status. Absence of a listed name does not establish nonuse. Unresolved source fields must not be interpreted as standardized measurements. Age is top-coded at 90. The sharing basis for the recorded minor and participants without recorded age remains under review. The public dictionary corrects the lipoprotein(a) unit to mg/L using the original header; values are unchanged.

References: Lang et al. (2015), DOI [10.1016/j.echo.2014.10.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.echo.2014.10.003), for the LVM convention. The data dictionary records processing limitations; computational agreement alone does not establish measurement validity, causal interpretation or representativeness.
